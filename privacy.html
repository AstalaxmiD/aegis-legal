Privacy Policy

Effective August 6, 2026
Aegis protects your photos on your device. Nothing you protect, verify, or compare ever leaves your phone.

Everything on your device

Aegis runs entirely on your iPhone. Any photo you protect, verify, or compare is processed on your device and is never uploaded anywhere. Aegis has no servers.

Data we collect

None. Aegis has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party analytics or advertising services. We collect no personal information of any kind, and we receive nothing from your device.

What stays on your phone

When you protect a photo, Aegis stores a protection record on your device: a small comparison copy, image fingerprints, and the signed credential for that photo. The signing keys and a small free-tier usage counter are stored in the iOS Keychain and, where available, the Secure Enclave. These records exist only on your phone.

Purchases

Aegis Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment through your Apple Account; Aegis never sees your name, payment details, or Apple Account information. The app only receives confirmation from the App Store, on your device, that a purchase is active. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Photo access

Aegis can only see the photos you pick. The photo library permission is used to protect and verify the photos you choose, and for nothing else.

Deletion and your control

Delete a protection record in the app, or use Delete All Data in Settings, and it is gone; delete the app and its documents are removed with it. Nothing needs deleting anywhere else, because nothing ever left your phone. Two small technical entries live in the iOS Keychain: the signing keys and the free-tier usage counter. iOS may keep Keychain entries in its secure storage after an app is deleted; they contain no personal information and cannot identify you. Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights over personal data an organization holds about you; we hold none, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, sell, share, or delete on our side, and no consent to revoke.

Children

Aegis is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. Aegis collects no personal information from any user. Photos you protect on your device, including photos of your family, never reach us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version ships with an app update and the effective date above changes with it.
